## Clearance

### IFR

| Situation                              | Phraseology                                                                                                                                                                                       | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Departures With an RNAV SID**        | **CLEARED TO (**_) AIRPORT, VIA (_**) DEPARTURE, THEN AS FILED, CLIMB VIA SID, EXPECT (**_) ONE ZERO MINUTES AFTER DEPARTURE, DEPARTURE FREQUENCY (_**), SQUAWK (\_\_\_).**                       | "Delta 327, cleared to Seattle Airport via the CUTTN2 departure, then as filed, climb via SID, expect FL380 one zero minutes after departure, departure frequency 133.47, squawk 3534."                      |
| **Departures With a Radar Vector SID** | **CLEARED TO (**_) AIRPORT, VIA (_**) DEPARTURE, RADAR VECTORS (**_), THEN AS FILED, MAINTAIN (_**), EXPECT (**_) ONE ZERO MINUTES AFTER DEPARTURE, DEPARTURE FREQUENCY (_**), SQUAWK (\_\_\_).** | "United 134, cleared to Denver Airport via the ORD9 departure, radar vectors OLINN then as filed, climb via SID, expect FL380 one zero minutes after departure, departure frequency 119.0, <br>squawk 4723." |
| **Departures Without a SID**           | **CLEARED TO (**_) AIRPORT, <br>VIA RADAR VECTORS (_**), THEN AS FILED, <br>MAINTAIN (**_), EXPECT (_**) ONE ZERO MINUTES AFTER DEPARTURE, <br>DEPARTURE FREQUENCY (**_), <br>SQUAWK (_**).**     | "Brickyard 3376, cleared to <br>Houston Airport via radar vectors DSM, then as filed, <br>maintain 5,000, expect FL360 one zero minutes after departure, <br>departure frequency 123.85, <br>squawk 7224."   |

| **Class D VFR Departure / Pattern** | **[ACID], [POSITION NAME], SQUAWK VFR/1200, CALL FOR TAXI/CONTACT GND FOR TAXI.**                        | "N239UD, Memphis Ground, squawk VFR, call for taxi."                                                      |
| **VFR Flight Following**            | **DEPARTURE FREQUENCY (\_\_\_).**                                                                        | "... departure frequency [FREQUENCY], squawk [CODE]."                                                     |

## Ground

### Fixed Wing

| Situation                          | Phraseology                                                                            | Example                                                                                               |
| :--------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Taxi to Departure Runway**       | **[ACID], [POSITION NAME], RUNWAY (**_), TAXI VIA (_**).**                             | "Delta 327, Memphis Ground, Runway [RUNWAY], taxi via [TAXI ROUTE]."                                  |
| **Taxi to Runway at Intersection** | **[ACID], [POSITION NAME], RUNWAY (**_) AT (_**), TAXI VIA (\_\_\_).**                 | "N445KB, Memphis Ground, Runway [RUNWAY] at [INTERSECTION], taxi via [TAXI ROUTE]."                   |
| **Taxi With Runway Hold Short**    | **[ACID], [POSITION NAME], RUNWAY (**_), TAXI VIA (_**), HOLD SHORT RUNWAY (\_\_\_).** | "Southwest 3328, Memphis Ground, Runway [RUNWAY], taxi via [TAXI ROUTE], hold short Runway [RUNWAY]." |
| **Continue Taxi**                  | **[ACID], [POSITION NAME], CROSS RUNWAY/TAXIWAY (**_), CONTINUE TAXI VIA (_**).**      | "Southwest 3328, cross Runway [RUNWAY], continue taxi via [TAXI ROUTE]."                              |
