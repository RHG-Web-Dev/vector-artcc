# KMEM Departure Cheatsheet

## Initial Altitude

- **Jets:** 5,000 or lower; RNAV SID
  - Non-RNAV: ELVIS4
- **Props:** 3,000 or lower; ELVIS4 SID
- **VFR:** At or below 2,500
- **Pattern:**
  - Props: 1,300
  - Jets: 1,800
