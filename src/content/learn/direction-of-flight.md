# Direction of Flight

The direction of flight determines the cruising altitude assigned to an IFR aircraft.

For IFR operations, cruising altitudes are generally based on the aircraft's **magnetic course**.

## Magnetic Course

Determine the aircraft's magnetic course by looking at the direction it will travel along the route.

The basic rule is:

| Magnetic Course | IFR Altitude   |
| --------------- | -------------- |
| 000°–179°       | Odd thousands  |
| 180°–359°       | Even thousands |
