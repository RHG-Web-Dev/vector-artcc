# Airways

Airways are published routes used to organize IFR traffic between navigation fixes, generally following defined navigation facilities or RNAV waypoints.

## VOR Airways

**Victor airways (Vxxx)** are low-altitude airways based primarily on VOR navigation.

Examples:

- V1
- V2
- V12
- V44

Victor airways are generally used below the high-altitude airway structure.

### Identification

Victor airway names begin with **V** followed by a number.

> **V12**

When reading an airway clearance, pronounce the airway designator and number using standard aviation phraseology.

---

## Jet Routes

**Jet routes (Jxxx)** are high-altitude IFR routes.

Examples:

- J45
- J73
- J110

Jet routes are generally associated with the high-altitude route structure and are used to move IFR traffic efficiently between fixes.

### Identification

Jet route names begin with **J** followed by a number.

> **J45**

---

## RNAV Routes

RNAV routes provide published IFR routing that does not necessarily rely on direct navigation between ground-based VOR facilities.

Common RNAV route designators include:

- **Txxx** — low-altitude RNAV routes
- **Qxxx** — high-altitude RNAV routes

Examples:

> **T207**

> **Q100**

Always verify the applicable altitude structure and published route before using an RNAV route.

---

## Route Structure

An airway clearance may contain multiple fixes connected by one or more airways.

Example:

```text
MEM → V18 → ABC → J42 → XYZ
```
