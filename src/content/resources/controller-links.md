## Flight Plan Suffixes

Flight plan equipment suffixes communicate aircraft capabilities and should be considered when evaluating procedures and route options. The commonly encountered legacy examples include `/L`, `/G`, and `/A`.

For additional background:

- [FlightAware: Aircraft Flight Plan Suffix FAQ](https://www.flightaware.com/about/faq_aircraft_flight_plan_suffix.rvt)

Always compare third-party references with the current VATSIM, FAA, and facility documentation before using them for an active session.
