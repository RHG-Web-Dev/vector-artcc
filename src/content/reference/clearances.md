# IFR Clearances

A practical reference for issuing, receiving, and amending IFR clearances.

Use this page as a quick reference while working clearance delivery or preparing for an IFR clearance.
<br><br>

---

<br><br>

## Picking Up a Clearance

When a pilot is ready to receive their IFR clearance, they should identify themselves and state that they are looking for their clearance.

### Pilot

```text
[CALLSIGN] Looking for IFR Clearance to [Airport].
```
