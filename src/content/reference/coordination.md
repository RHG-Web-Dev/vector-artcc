# Coordination

Coordination should be brief, complete, and easy for the receiving controller to understand. A useful crossing request answers four questions: **who**, **how many**, **what**, and **where**.

## Crossing Coordination

A simple memory aid is:

**WHO → WHO AM I → HOW MANY → WHAT → WHERE**

### Example

```text
Local, Ground, one to cross Runway 27 at Sierra.
```

Breakdown:

- **Who:** Local
- **Who am I:** Ground
- **How many:** One
- **What:** Cross Runway 27
- **Where:** Sierra

### Completion

After the crossing is complete:

```text
Local, Ground, crossing complete.
```

## Practical Notes

When working a position, know which crossings or releases require coordination before issuing the instruction. Facility-specific practices, runway configurations, and local SOPs take precedence over this general reference.
